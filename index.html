Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about our services and financial matters

For small businesses, we offer a comprehensive suite of services including bookkeeping, tax preparation and planning, financial statement preparation, payroll processing, business advisory, and retirement planning. Our goal is to provide you with the financial clarity and strategy needed to grow your business while managing compliance requirements efficiently.

We recommend meeting with your accountant at least quarterly to review financial performance, discuss tax planning opportunities, and address any issues before they become problems. However, the frequency can vary based on your business needs and complexity. For startups and businesses in transition, monthly meetings may be more appropriate, while established businesses with stable operations might meet semi-annually in addition to tax season.

While all CPAs are accountants, not all accountants are CPAs. A Certified Public Accountant (CPA) has met specific education and experience requirements, passed a rigorous examination, and maintains continuing education to keep their license. CPAs have additional qualifications that allow them to perform certain tasks that regular accountants cannot, such as representing clients before the IRS, conducting audits, and providing attestation services. At FinancePro, all our senior advisors are licensed CPAs.

We offer several pricing models to accommodate different client needs. For routine services like tax preparation or bookkeeping, we typically charge fixed fees that are agreed upon in advance. For more complex advisory services or business consulting, we may use hourly rates or value-based pricing. We believe in transparent pricing and will discuss all fees upfront before beginning any work, so you'll never encounter unexpected charges.

For individual tax preparation, you should bring income statements (W-2s, 1099s), investment income records, property tax statements, mortgage interest statements, charitable contribution receipts, and any major life event documentation (marriage, new child, home purchase). Business owners should also bring profit and loss statements, balance sheets, asset purchase records, and business expense documentation. We provide clients with a detailed checklist appropriate to their specific situation before tax season to ensure a smooth process.
